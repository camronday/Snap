Snap Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 September 2026

No data collection

Snap does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data or screen content. Everything you capture stays on your Mac.

Screen Recording permission

Snap requests Screen Recording access solely to capture your screen when you trigger a capture. Captured images and GIFs are placed on your clipboard or saved to a file you choose. They are never sent off-device.

Clipboard access

Snap reads your clipboard only when you explicitly choose "Annotate Clipboard Image". It writes to your clipboard when you complete a capture or copy annotated output. No clipboard contents are logged or transmitted.

Temporary files

GIF recordings are written to your Mac's temporary directory while recording. Snap deletes them when a new recording replaces them and on every launch and quit.

No analytics or crash reporting

Snap contains no analytics, telemetry, crash reporters, or advertising SDKs. It makes no network connections.

Contact

Questions? Open an issue at github.com/camronday/Snap.
